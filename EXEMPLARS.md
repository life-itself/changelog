# Exemplars

Reference changelogs, kept for shaping this spec — not part of it, not linked from `CONVENTION.md` or `add-to-agents.md`, no project-repo agent should read this while drafting an entry.

## linear.app/changelog

The reference point for the "something bigger" tier in `CONVENTION.md`. What it does well:
- Real length variation — some entries are a sentence with a screenshot, others are several paragraphs walking through a feature with multiple images and a short video.
- Always shows the thing, not just describes it — screenshots/GIFs of the actual UI are the default, not the exception.
- Links out to deeper docs/release notes when there's more to say, rather than cramming everything into the entry itself.
- Groups related changes under one dated entry rather than one entry per commit — reads as "here's what shipped," not a commit log.

## What to borrow vs. not

Borrow: length variation tied to substance, always-link-the-thing, image-forward for anything visual.

Don't borrow: Linear's changelog is a product-marketing surface with a design team behind it — polish level and cadence (near-daily, professionally illustrated) isn't the bar for a solo/small-project changelog. The point is the *judgment* (show, don't just tell; match length to substance), not matching production values.
